# Product requirements and priority

Source of requested scope: user-supplied master directive and advanced expansion A–N.

P0: tenant identity, policy enforcement, provider abstraction, durable task DAG, task ownership and leases, approval gates, budgets, evidence verification, audit events.
P1: persistent memory, hierarchical agents, secure tool runner, repo worker, branch/PR workflow.
P2: company generator creating persisted resources, research, sales/marketing agents, mission control.
P3: CRM, finance, digital twin, anomaly detection, remediation, marketplace, SaaS billing.

Only a narrow portion of P0 is implemented in this package. Provider-connected AI autonomy requires configured credentials and additional software.
